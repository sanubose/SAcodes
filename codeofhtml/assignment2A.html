The word "teach"

The word "teach" means to instruct, train, or give someone knowledge of something. For example, "I taught for a few years before becoming a lawyer". It can also mean to cause someone to know how to do something, such as "is teaching me to drive"

Teach For India

Teach For India is a non-profit organization that aims to provide all children in India with an excellent education. The organization was founded in 2008 by Shaheen Mistri and is part of the Teach For All network.
